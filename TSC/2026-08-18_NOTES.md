## Next major release and communication

The next major OpenTofu release is targeted for September, aligned with the Go release. The shorter release cycle and experimental features require a different communication approach: maintainers are considering separate, more frequent posts about individual in-progress features, potentially monthly, rather than only release-focused posts.

## Maintainer role proposal

Laurence Bordowitz presented [PR #53](https://github.com/opentofu/org/pull/53), which adds the organization-level Maintainer role, distinguishes it from repository-level maintainership, and defines changes to Committer and Maintainer status.

The discussion requested clearer definitions of the Contributor, Committer, and Maintainer roles, including their expectations and direct-write/CI privileges. The group preferred expectations over punitive language and noted that role scope may differ across repositories. Laurence will revise the proposal; remaining discussion will continue in the pull request, with the goal of bringing a revised proposal to a future TSC vote.
